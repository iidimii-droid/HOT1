### Original documentary VO (script.md, for comparison)
> On an island in the Galápagos, a finch snaps a twig from a branch, tests it, discards it for
> being too short, and selects another. She works it into a crack in the bark until a grub comes
> out. She is using a tool.

**Why the change:** same facts (spine held lengthwise, too-short twigs rejected, grub extracted),
shorter sentences for kids, and the rejected-twig fact becomes Caw's first gag — which sets up the
series thesis: animals *use* tools; only humans *imagine* new ones.
