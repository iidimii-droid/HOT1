IRONFALL

Squad tactics in armored war. Take the ground. Hold the line.

Armor scheme

This is the single-player version, so it runs in your browser against the AI. Online matches need the Ironfall server (see the README). You need a mouse and keyboard to play.

How to play
  • Win by holding more of the 3 victory points (stars) than the enemy until their tickets drain to 0, or by destroying the enemy HQ (right-click it with squads selected). Tanks and Lance teams hit it hardest.
  • Left-click / drag to select squads. Right-click to move, or right-click an enemy to attack.
  • Rally point: left-click your HQ (or press H), then right-click the map. New units walk there.
  • A (or Q) then click: attack-move, and every soldier fires at the nearest enemy in range. E stop. Esc cancels the last unit in production. R retreat to HQ (takes half damage). T reinforce at HQ.
  • G then click: squad ability (frag grenade, jump pack).
  • Squads engage on sight: when any soldier spots an enemy, the whole squad opens fire and closes in (unless it is in cover or is a heavy weapon team).
  • Capture R (requisition) and P (power) points to fund your army. Build with Z X C V B.
  • Cover: order a squad next to sandbags, walls or ruins and the soldiers spread out behind them (green shield). Craters give light cover (yellow).
  • Heavy Support teams suppress and pin infantry. Use Lance teams against tanks.
  • Camera: W A S D or arrow keys (A also starts attack-move when squads are selected), hold right-click and drag, screen edges, or click the minimap. Mouse wheel zooms. Ctrl+1–9 sets groups.
