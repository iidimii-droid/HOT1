IRONFALL

Squad tactics in armored war. Take the ground. Hold the line.

This is the single-player version, so it runs in your browser against the AI. Online matches need the Ironfall server (see the README). You need a mouse and keyboard to play.

How to play
  • Win by holding more of the 3 victory points (stars) than the enemy. Their tickets drain until 0.
  • Left-click / drag to select squads. Right-click to move, or right-click an enemy to attack.
  • A then click: attack-move. S stop. R retreat to HQ (takes half damage). T reinforce at HQ.
  • G then click: squad ability (frag grenade, jump pack).
  • Capture R (requisition) and P (power) points to fund your army. Build with Z X C V B.
  • Stay near sandbags, walls and ruins (green shield) or in craters (yellow) for cover.
  • Heavy Support teams suppress and pin infantry. Use Lance teams against tanks.
  • Camera: arrow keys, screen edges, middle-drag, mouse wheel to zoom. Ctrl+1–9 sets groups.
